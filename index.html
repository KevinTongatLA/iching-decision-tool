决策时机工具 Decision-Timing Tool — Pilot: BaZi + Current Moment

Scope of this pilot: Four Pillars (八字) for a person, and the same engine applied to the current moment (环境/当下). No I-Ching hexagram/timing layer yet — that's the next step. This is a codified traditional heuristic system, not a validated predictive model.

命主 — Person

Uses local civil clock time as entered — no true-solar-time / longitude correction yet (a refinement for later; matters most near hour-pillar boundaries).

时刻 — Moment (default: now)

Pick any date/time to see its Four Pillars, and — once you've computed the person above — how that moment compares to them.

起卦 — Cast a Hexagram for this Moment

Deterministic 梅花易数 time-based casting (年月日时起卦法) from the Moment panel's date/time above — no randomness. Lunar month/day are computed using China Standard Time civil dates regardless of the Moment panel's timezone field, matching classical practice. If the person's Four Pillars are computed, each line is also checked against their Day Master and day branch.

寻找更好时机 — Scan for a better time

Requires the person's Four Pillars computed above. Scans forward from the Moment panel's current date/time and ranks candidates by the composite score below (see the "关于评分" note under the results) — a starting point for comparison, not a verdict.

校验 — Day-pillar offset: confirmed

Cross-checked against an online 黄历 for two dates 8 days apart (2026-08-24 → 庚午, 2026-09-01 → 戊寅) — both matched this tool's output, so GANZHI_JD_OFFSET = 49 is correct. Since day-pillar computation is just a continuous day count (not tied to those specific dates), this confidence extends to other dates too. The manual override below is kept as a fallback only.
